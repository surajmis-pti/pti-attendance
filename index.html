Attendance Report

Galat entry ho to ✕ se hatao. IN/OUT ka time galat ho to "✎ IN" / "✎ OUT" se sahi karo. Sheet mein apne aap theek ho jayega.

Row wise manual entry

Har row mein employee, IN/OUT, date aur time bharo. Ek hi employee ki kai rows (alag din ya IN + OUT) bhi chalengi. "+ Row jodo" se nayi row aati hai, phir neeche button se sab ek saath lag jayengi.
Us din ka pehle se laga IN (ya OUT) ho to naye time se badal jayega. Jo row lag jayegi wo list se hat jayegi; jo nahi lagi uske saath wajah dikhegi.
